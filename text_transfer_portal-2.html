How to use it
1
On your mobile

Open this page, enter your shift log or other text, then tap Copy text or Save .txt.

2
Get it to your office PC

Use an organisation-approved route to access this same page or move the saved text file to the PC.

3
Copy into your work document

On the PC, open the text and paste it into your shift log or approved application.

Important: this is a front-end prototypeThis page does not send or store text on a server, and opening it on another device will not automatically transfer your text. Cross-device transfer needs an approved shared server or a one-time code service. Do not enter confidential plant or operational information into a public website without authorisation.
